RINDHALI THODDOO — DESIGN RESEARCH & UPGRADE NOTES
==================================================

Sources reviewed
- Hooray L.A.B., "12 Must-Follow Hotel Website Design Trends in 2026"
- ScaleMyHotel, "Hotel Website Design Best Practices" (updated 2026)
- Noovy, "How to Create an Effective Hotel Website in 2026"
- HotelTechReport and Awwwards hotel/resort showcases

Key findings and what was applied
1. Mobile first (60–70% of hotel traffic is mobile)
   -> Sticky bottom booking bar on phones ("Check availability" + WhatsApp), within thumb reach,
      hidden while the menu is open. Floating glass header, large tap targets.
2. Visible booking widget + direct-booking perks next to it
   -> Booking bar in the hero, with three concrete perks underneath:
      no booking-site fees · personal WhatsApp service · transfers & meal plans in one request.
3. Social proof near rooms
   -> A real guest quote and score on every room card; 10-review testimonial slider.
4. Transparency
   -> Plain-language policy line on each room (cash payment, check-in/out, children, beds).
5. Accessibility
   -> Skip-to-content link, visible focus, labelled form fields, pause/play buttons on the
      hero slideshow and review slider, reduced-motion support, Arabic right-to-left layout.
6. Local context
   -> Map plus a walking-times list (restaurants, island centre, beach) on the Contact page.
7. Speed
   -> Hero image loads first (fetchpriority), lazy-loaded images elsewhere, no heavy libraries.
8. Privacy
   -> Privacy notice page, linked from the form and footer.
9. Immersive storytelling
   -> Parallax hero, cinematic photo reveals, scroll-progress line, live Thoddoo weather
      and Maldives time in the top bar.

Brand
- Palette "Lagoon & Palm": cream #FFFAF2, deep lagoon #006D77, palm lime #9BD35A,
  aqua #83C5BE, ink #12302E.
- Logo: a Maldivian dhoni (traditional curved-prow boat) sailing at sunset inside an island
  arch, crowned with a frangipani flower. Files: logo.svg (full), logo-mark.svg (symbol),
  logo-seal.svg (round stamp for social media, menus, signage).

Languages
- English, Arabic (right-to-left), Russian, German, French, Italian.
- Fully translated: menu, top bar, footer, mobile bar, the whole Home page, the whole Contact
  page and enquiry form, and every page's main heading, introduction and call to action.
- Detailed body text on inner pages (room details, dining, Thoddoo guide, FAQ, privacy) is in
  English for now. Guest reviews stay in their original wording.
- To add or correct a translation: edit i18n.js (one line per phrase).

Enquiry form
- Full country list, separate adults and children, and an age box per child (under 1 to 17).
- The guesthouse receives the request in English with the guest's language noted (e.g. "Language: RU").

Responsive check
- All 9 pages tested at 375 px (phone), 768 px (tablet) and 1280 px (desktop) in English,
  Arabic, Russian and German: no sideways scrolling, menu on one line on desktop.
