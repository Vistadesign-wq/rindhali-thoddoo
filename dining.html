The restaurant

Generous plates, fresh island produce

“Good breakfast, lunch and dinner, with plenty of variety and fresh produce.”

Our on-site restaurant serves breakfast every morning, plus lunch and dinner for guests on half or full board. Prefer to eat on your balcony? Room service is available, and we’re happy to point you to Thoddoo’s cafés a few steps away.
